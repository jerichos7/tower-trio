Tower Trio Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Tower Trio (“the game”) uses, why it uses it, who receives it and what choices you have. It covers the game on Android phones (from Google Play) and on iPhone (from Apple's App Store). The game is made by Sarp Aras (“we”, “us”). The short version comes first; the details follow.

In short

  • There is no account. The game never asks for your name, email address or phone number, and it does not read your photos, contacts or precise location.
  • Your progress, coins and settings are saved only on your phone. We have no server that stores them.
  • The game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay (ad networks: Unity Ads and ironSource Ads). To show an ad, these services receive information about your device, such as its advertising identifier and IP address. On iPhone, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is only available if you allow tracking in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • Unity Analytics tells us how levels are played, for example where players get stuck, so we can balance the game. It only runs if you allow it; on iPhone it also needs your permission in Apple's tracking prompt.
  • Purchases go through Google Play on Android and through Apple's App Store on iPhone. We never see your card or bank details.
  • The first time you open the game, before any ads load, a notice tells you what the ad and analytics services receive (on Android, including information about other apps installed on your phone), and you choose whether to allow personalized ads and analytics. On iPhone, if you agree, Apple's tracking prompt then asks for your permission as well. You can change your answers at any time in the game's Settings and, on iPhone, in the phone's Settings.

1. Who is responsible

Sarp Aras is responsible for the personal data processed through the game (the “data controller” under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the “veri sorumlusu” under Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK)). You can reach us at sarparas34@hotmail.com.

2. Information saved on your phone

The game saves your level progress and stars, coins, boosters, lives, the one-time items you bought, your settings (sound, music, vibration, language, color symbols) and your privacy choice. This save stays in the game's private storage on your phone. It is not sent to us.

If you delete the game or clear its data, the save is deleted for good. We keep no copy of it. Your phone's own backup (for example iCloud on iPhone, or Google's backup on Android, if you have it turned on) may include the save, and restoring that backup on a new phone can bring it back; that backup is handled by Apple or Google, not by us.

3. Ads (Unity LevelPlay)

Ads keep the game free. The game shows rewarded ads that you choose to watch (for example for an extra life, a second chance in a level or free coins) and, unless you have bought “Remove Ads”, full-screen ads between levels: only after a level is won, from level 20 on, at most after every third win and never during a level.

The ads are served by Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service of Unity Technologies, with the ad networks Unity Ads and ironSource Ads (ironSource is part of Unity). LevelPlay includes Unity's Ad Quality component, which checks the ads that are shown for quality and fraud and receives the same kinds of information. If we add other ad networks, we will list them here first.

What the ad services receive

  • Your device's identifiers: on Android the advertising ID and the App Set ID; on iPhone the IDFV (an ID shared only by apps from the same developer on your phone) and, only if you allow tracking, the IDFA (Apple's advertising identifier). Also other installation identifiers made by the ad software.
  • Your IP address, and the approximate location worked out from it (country, region, city). Not your precise GPS location.
  • Device and app details: phone model, operating system and version, language, screen size, network type, the game's version.
  • How you interact with ads: which ads were shown, taps on them, whether a rewarded ad was watched to the end.
  • Diagnostic information about how ads load and perform.
  • On Android, depending on the version of the ad software, information about other apps installed on your phone, which ad networks use to prevent fraud and choose ads. (iPhone does not give apps this list.)
  • On Android, the ad software can also use the advertising features of Android's Privacy Sandbox (ad topics and ad measurement) where your phone offers them. You control these in your phone's settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or “Ad privacy”).

Why

To show ads, measure them, limit how often you see the same ad, give you the reward of a rewarded ad and prevent fraud. If you allow it, the ad networks also use this information to show ads that match your interests (personalized ads), including information they have collected in other apps. On iPhone this also needs your permission in Apple's tracking prompt (see section 8).

On iPhone, ad networks can also measure whether an ad led to an install through Apple's SKAdNetwork and AdAttributionKit, which report results to them without identifying you or your device.

If you do not allow it, you still see ads, but they are not personalized, and the game asks the ad services not to sell or share your information for targeted ads. The ad services may still receive the information listed above to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud; how much of it they then process is decided by their own software and described in Unity's policy below.

Unity decides itself how it uses data for its ad services. Its policy explains this: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

4. Analytics (Unity Analytics)

Unity Analytics only runs if you allow it in the privacy choice. On iPhone it also runs only if you allow tracking in Apple's prompt (see section 8). It sends:

  • a random installation ID made by Unity (not your name, and not linked to an account);
  • game events: a level started, won or lost (and why), boosters used, coins earned and spent, shop visits, and purchases (which item and its price, never payment details);
  • when a session starts and ends, and how long it lasts;
  • device details (model, operating system, language; on iPhone also the IDFV) and the country worked out from your IP address;
  • performance and diagnostic data.

We use it to understand how the game is played and to improve it, for example to make a level easier if most players lose it. Unity processes this data for us as our service provider. We see it as statistics in Unity's dashboard and do not use it to find out who you are. We do not use analytics data for advertising, and we do not combine it with data from other companies' apps or websites. Unity's own privacy disclosure for its analytics software on iPhone, however, lists the device identifier and purchase history as data that may be used for tracking. For that reason, on iPhone analytics stays off unless you allow tracking in Apple's prompt.

5. Purchases (Google Play and the App Store)

Coin packs, the starter pack and “Remove Ads” are bought through Google Play's billing system on Android and through Apple's App Store (In-App Purchase) on iPhone. The game reaches both through Unity's in-app purchasing library. According to Unity's privacy disclosure for iPhone, that library also sends Unity some information so that purchases work and can be measured: what was bought (the product and its price), installation and device identifiers (on iPhone the IDFV, not the IDFA), the steps of a purchase in the shop, and usage and diagnostic information. This is not used for tracking. Google and Apple handle the payment under their own privacy policies (Google, Apple). We receive only what is needed to deliver the item and to restore “Remove Ads”: the product, an order or transaction number and the store's proof of purchase (a purchase token or receipt). We never see your card or bank details.

To get “Remove Ads” back on a new phone, use the game's Restore Purchases button while signed in with the same Google account or Apple Account you bought it with.

6. If you write to us

If you email us, we use your email address and your message only to answer you. We delete them at the latest 12 months after the matter is closed, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

7. What we do not do

  • No accounts, and no name, email address or phone number collected in the game.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or precise location.
  • No chat and no way to contact other players.
  • We do not sell your personal information for money.

8. Your privacy choice, and how to change it

The first time you open the game, before the ad and analytics software starts, the game shows a privacy notice. It names the information described in sections 3 and 4, including, on Android, information about other apps installed on your phone, says what it is used for, and asks whether you allow personalized ads and analytics. The ad software starts only after you answer, and analytics only if you agree.

  • Agree: ads may be personalized, and Unity Analytics runs. On iPhone, both also need your permission in Apple's tracking prompt (see below).
  • Decline: ads are not personalized, Unity Analytics does not run, and the game asks the ad services not to sell or share your information for targeted ads. You still see ads, and the ad services still receive some information about your device, which on Android can include information about installed apps, to deliver ads and prevent fraud, as their own policies describe. On iPhone, the game then does not show Apple's tracking prompt and does not track you.

You can change your answer at any time: open Settings (the gear on the map) and then Privacy. Withdrawing your consent stops the processing from then on; it does not make earlier processing unlawful. When you turn analytics off, the game also asks Unity to delete the analytics data linked to your device.

Other choices you have:

  • On Android, reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (usually Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads). On iPhone, use the tracking setting described below.
  • Buying “Remove Ads” stops full-screen ads. Rewarded ads stay optional; you only see one when you choose to.
  • Inside a Unity ad, the privacy icon lets you opt out of some personalized advertising.
  • To report an ad that is inappropriate or broken, use “Report an ad” in the game's Settings, or email us. Tell us what the ad showed and when you saw it.

On iPhone: Apple's tracking prompt (App Tracking Transparency)

On iPhone, “tracking” means linking information from the game with information from other companies' apps and websites for targeted ads or ad measurement. Apple lets an app track you only with your permission. If you choose Agree in the game's notice, iPhone then shows Apple's prompt asking whether Tower Trio may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites:

  • Allow: the ad services may use the IDFA and the information in section 3 to personalize and measure ads across apps, and Unity Analytics runs.
  • Ask App Not to Track: the IDFA is not available to the game or the ad services (iPhone gives them only zeros), and the game asks the ad services for ads that are not personalized, as with Decline. Unity Analytics does not run either.

If you choose Decline in the game's notice, the game does not show Apple's prompt and does not track you. You can change your answer at any time in the iPhone's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, or in Settings > Tower Trio. If apps are not allowed to ask to track on your iPhone (a switch on that Tracking page, which Apple also turns off for children's accounts), the game never asks, never tracks you and keeps analytics off. No feature, reward or price in the game depends on your answer.

9. Why we are allowed to use the data (legal bases)

  • Your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); KVKK Art. 5(1), explicit consent): personalized ads and analytics.
  • Performing our agreement with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b); KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)): delivering and restoring what you buy.
  • Our legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)): non-personalized ads that keep the game free, preventing fraud and keeping the game secure.

The data is collected automatically, through the software of the services named above that is built into the game.

10. Transfers to other countries

Unity, Google and Apple process data in the United States and other countries outside Turkey and the European Economic Area. These transfers rely on the safeguards the law provides, such as standard contractual clauses under the GDPR and the safeguards set out in Article 9 of KVKK.

11. How long the data is kept

  • The save on your phone: until you delete the game or its data.
  • Analytics data: kept by Unity only as long as needed for analytics under Unity's retention rules, then deleted or anonymized. You can have it deleted earlier (see section 13).
  • Ad data: kept by Unity and its ad networks as long as needed for the purposes above and their legal duties (such as fraud prevention), as their policies describe.
  • Emails to us: at most 12 months after the matter is closed.

12. Children

Tower Trio is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are under the age at which you can give consent online in your country (between 13 and 16 in the EU), ask a parent before you answer the privacy question. If you are a parent and believe your child has used the game, write to us and we will help delete the data linked to their device.

13. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its processing (including profiling for ads), to data portability and to withdraw your consent at any time.

Under Article 11 of KVKK you have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed and to ask for information about it; to learn the purpose of the processing and whether the data is used for that purpose; to know the third parties in Turkey or abroad to whom it is transferred; to have incomplete or wrong data corrected; to have it deleted or destroyed; to ask that these corrections and deletions are passed on to those third parties; to object to a result against you that comes only from automated analysis; and to claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

To use any of these rights, email sarparas34@hotmail.com. We answer free of charge within 30 days. Because we do not know your name, we may ask a few questions so that we can find the data that belongs to your device.

You can also complain to a data protection authority: in Turkey the Personal Data Protection Authority (kvkk.gov.tr), in the EU the authority of your country.

How to delete your data

  • On your phone: on Android, uninstall the game or clear its data (phone Settings > Apps > Tower Trio > Storage > Clear data); on iPhone, delete the app (touch and hold its icon > Remove App > Delete App). A copy may remain in your phone's own backup until that backup is replaced or deleted.
  • Analytics: in the game, open Settings > Privacy and turn analytics off. The game then asks Unity to delete the analytics data linked to your device. You can also email us.
  • Ads: reset or delete your advertising ID (Android) or turn off tracking for the game (iPhone, Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking), and contact Unity through the privacy contact in its Game Player and App User Privacy Policy, or email us and we will pass your request on.

14. Notice for United States residents

Some US state laws, such as California's, call giving data to ad networks for targeted ads a “sale” or “sharing”, even when no money changes hands. Choosing Decline in the game's privacy choice opts you out of this. You can also email us to use your rights under these laws.

15. Security

The services named above send data over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The save on your phone sits in the game's private storage, which other apps cannot read. We keep no copy of it on a server.

16. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top. We announce important changes in the game or on its Google Play and App Store pages, and if a change needs your consent, the game asks you again.
